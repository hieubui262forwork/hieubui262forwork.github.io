03 — Process

From waiting
to delivering.

Most of a good landscape frame happens before the shutter: knowing where the sun will set, and being there twenty minutes early.

01

Scout

Track sunset direction and the bloom calendar. Return to the same spot across days until the light, crowd and sky cooperate.

02

Capture

Shoot RAW on the Canon EOS R50. Expose for the highlights, let silhouettes go dark, and use a long focal length to compress the skyline.

03

Grade

Lightroom: warm amber highlights, olive-green shadows, lifted blacks and fine grain — one consistent look across every series.

04

Deliver

Crop and export per use: square masters for streaming covers, 4:5 for feeds, 9:16 for TikTok, with safe space for titles.

04 — Critique

What 435K likes
taught me about a strong image.

Posting to a real audience is a constant A/B test. These are the patterns I use to judge my own work — and anyone else’s.

246.6Kvs ~1–7K

One clear light event beats a pretty scene.

A single, readable moment — the city going dark under a glowing sky — outperformed technically clean night streets by more than 30×. Viewers need to know what to look at within a second.

163.9Kviews

Specific and seasonal beats generic.

A street of white blossom says this week, this city. Timeliness gives people a reason to share and save, which a beautiful but timeless sunset rarely does.

168.8Kviews

Texture and mood carry a frame without a landmark.

Rain beading on a green railing has no famous subject, yet it held attention. Craft — focus, tone, a restrained palette — can be the whole story.
